# Sharma News

The daily news hub for **Sharma Electric & HVAC** — live at https://sharmaelechvac.github.io/sharma-news/

- **Home** (`docs/index.html`) — today's Legal and Finance edition cards + archives for both streams.
- **Finance Briefing** (`docs/finance/`) — the latest full daily finance briefing, with a dated archive (`docs/finance/archive/YYYY-MM-DD.html`) back to Nov 11, 2026.
- **Legal Briefing** — lives on its own site: https://sharmaelechvac.github.io/sharma-legal-briefing/ (linked from the hub; archive pages at `/archive/YYYY-MM-DD.html`).

Updated daily at 5:00 AM ET. Pages are generated by `build.py` from the daily briefing reports in `~/workspace/research_notes/` — no content is invented; every item keeps its numbers and source URLs.
