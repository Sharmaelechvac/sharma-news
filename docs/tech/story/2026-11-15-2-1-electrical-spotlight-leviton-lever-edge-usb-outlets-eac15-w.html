  • Maker: Leviton (Leviton Manufacturing Co., Melville, N.Y.)
  • Release date: Thu Nov 12, 2026 — Leviton press release dated Nov 12, 2026 (teased Nov 2 on Leviton's channels; the formal announcement is Nov 12, in this edition's window).
  • What it is: Four new duplex receptacles that combine Leviton's Lever Edge lever-termination wiring with built-in USB charging. Two tamper-resistant receptacles stay free while two USB ports charge devices. Two configurations: USB Type-A + Type-C at 27W total (EAC15-W 15A / EAC20-W 20A), or dual USB Type-C at 30W total — up to 15W per port (ECC15-W / ECC20-W). Charge-only ports (no data), smart chip that optimises charging power, overload protection.
  • Why it's cool for Sharma: Lever Edge is the speed play: open the colour-coded lever, push the stripped wire in, click it shut — Leviton rates wiring at up to 3.5× faster than traditional screw-terminal devices, with an audible click confirming termination, no exposed metal parts, and no tape wrap needed. Dual hot/neutral terminals simplify feed-through, the 15A models support 20A feed-through, and tongue-and-groove straps keep multi-gang trim-out straight. For a residential RNC and trim-out operation, USB outlets are also exactly the kind of visible, homeowner-pleasing upgrade builders can sell — now without the slow install. Accepts #14–#12 AWG (15A models) / #12–#10 AWG (20A models), solid or stranded copper; note it requires a deep wallbox. Available in white through Leviton's normal channels.
  • Price: Not published in Leviton's release — no price stated here (do not quote one).
