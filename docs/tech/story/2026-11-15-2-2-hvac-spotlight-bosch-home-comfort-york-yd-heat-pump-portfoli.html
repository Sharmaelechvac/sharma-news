  • Maker: Bosch Home Comfort Group (Robert Bosch GmbH), under its YORK® brand; matching YORK® JD series air handlers; sister portfolios launched under Coleman®, Champion®, Luxaire® and Fraser-Johnson®.
  • Release date: Mon Nov 2, 2026 — Bosch Home Comfort Group launch release dated Dallas, Nov 2, 2026 (the newest verifiable North American residential HVAC launch found; trade coverage ran Nov 3–13, including ACHR News Nov 4 and pv magazine Nov 10/13). Labelled honestly: it is ~2 weeks old, not a this-week launch.
  • What it is: A full residential split-system heat pump line — 15.2 SEER2 / 8.5 HSPF2 up to 20 SEER2 / 10 HSPF2 — in which every tier, including entry level, gets a fully modulating inverter compressor, instead of variable-speed being a premium-only feature. Cold-climate models hold heating performance down to –13°F (–25°C). Factory-matched JD air handlers remove indoor/outdoor pairing guesswork; dual-fuel compatible; sound as low as 56 dBA (low-noise blades, isolating mounts); stainless heat exchangers; on-board diagnostics; fully communicating with the YORK Smart Home Control SHC350 thermostat and GoTemp Home app, with contractor resources in the GoTemp Pro app. 10-year limited warranty.
  • Why it's cool for Sharma: One line covers the whole good-better-best conversation with a builder or homeowner — variable-capacity comfort and humidity control (a real Florida selling point) at an entry price tier, fewer callbacks from on/off cycling, and simpler system matching for crews. For Sharma's HVAC side, a single-brand, every-tier-inverter lineup simplifies quoting, stocking and training.
  • Price: Bosch has not disclosed pricing in its launch announcement — no price stated here.

  • Source: Bosch launch release as published by Mechanical Hub, Nov 3, 2026 — https://mechanical-hub.com/bosch-launches-new-variable-capacity-heat-pump-portfolio-simplifying-system-selection/ ; pv magazine, Nov 10, 2026 — https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/11/10/bosch-unveils-residential-air-to-air-heat-pumps/ ; product family page — https://york.bosch-hcgroup.com/residential-equipment/heating-and-cooling/split-system-heat-pumps

  • Runner-up, also new: Rinnai REHPW series heat pump water heaters (50/65/80 gal, UEF 3.75–4.0, Wi-Fi, CTA-2045 demand-response certified), launched in coverage dated Nov 14, 2026 (Electrek) — a strong changeout/retrofit product, held as runner-up because water heating sits at the edge of Sharma's HVAC scope.
