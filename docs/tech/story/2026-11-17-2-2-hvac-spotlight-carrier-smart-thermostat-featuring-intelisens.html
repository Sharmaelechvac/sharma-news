  • Maker: Carrier (Carrier Global Corporation), co-developed with Universal Electronics Inc. (UEI)
  • Release date: Mon Nov 9, 2026 — joint announcement via Business Wire dated Nov 9, 2026. Labelled honestly: it is the newest verifiable U.S. residential HVAC product release not already featured in Editions 1–2, not a this-week announcement.
  • What it is: A smart thermostat that serves as the central control hub for the home's heating and cooling system, combining intelligent equipment control, remote diagnostics, energy management and guided commissioning in one device. With homeowner consent, InteliSense gives participating dealers real-time visibility into system health and performance for remote troubleshooting and fewer unnecessary service calls. Paired with Carrier's Performance 27VPA9 variable-speed heat pump, its patent-pending ComfortAdjust technology automatically manages up to 21 adaptive stages of heating and cooling for precise comfort and humidity control. The SmartSave energy management engine adapts equipment operation during peak utility demand periods; homeowners control the system through the Carrier SmartHome app (iOS/Android) with major smart-home platform compatibility and secure over-the-air updates. For technicians, the platform offers guided digital commissioning for faster installs and simplified smart-home pairing.
  • Why it's cool for Sharma: This is the service-model thermostat: diagnostics that reach the dealer before the truck rolls, guided commissioning that cuts install errors, and demand-response built in rather than bolted on. For Sharma's HVAC service and changeout work, a thermostat that reports system health with the homeowner's consent means better first-visit fix rates and a proactive-maintenance conversation builders and homeowners can actually see. The 21-stage ComfortAdjust pairing also gives crews a concrete humidity-control story for Florida homes when the thermostat is matched to Carrier's variable-speed heat pump.
