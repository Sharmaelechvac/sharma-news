Why it matters to Sharma: No direct Florida exposure, but the concreting/formwork point lands close to home — Sharma crews pour and set equipment pads, meter bases and underground work around structural pours. If the pour or the formwork next to your work looks wrong, saying so early…

  • What happened: Xinhua via Capital FM reported Sun Nov 15, 2026 that Kenyan authorities had accounted for all 405 workers after an eight-storey building under construction near Nairobi caved in at about 9:52 AM local time on Fri Nov 13, killing six people and injuring five. Nairobi East county commissioner Patrick Simiyu Were said preliminary reports indicate the upper section gave way during concreting works, with suspected formwork failure cited as a possible contributing factor; the site was closed pending structural assessments. A second incident followed on Sat Nov 14, when a perimeter wall at a Riverside construction site collapsed. On Sun Nov 15 the Institution of Engineers of Kenya called for urgent nationwide construction safety audits and independent investigations, linking the two incidents to the Oct 23 Eastleigh collapse that killed eight. Causes are not yet determined in either November incident.
  • Why it matters: A formwork/concreting failure during a pour, followed within a day by a second collapse in the same metro, is the international version of the same lesson as item 1.1: temporary-works discipline and inspection enforcement are where construction safety is actually decided.
  • Sharma impact: No direct Florida exposure, but the concreting/formwork point lands close to home — Sharma crews pour and set equipment pads, meter bases and underground work around structural pours. If the pour or the formwork next to your work looks wrong, saying so early is the job, not an interruption.
