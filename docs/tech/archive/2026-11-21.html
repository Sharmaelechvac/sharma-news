At a Glance

  • Construction starts: October construction starts rebounded 22.8% to a $1.63 trillion annual rate (Dodge Construction Network, reported Fri Nov 20) — but the gain was one sector deep: Tesla's $16.8 billion Terafab semiconductor plant in Texas led a manufacturing surge, and excluding manufacturing, total starts fell 1.1%. Residential starts fell 6.9%, with multifamily down 14.8%.
  • Update — permitting reform: The U.S. Senate passed the Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act late Thu Nov 19 by 62–33 (ENR, Nov 20) — the permitting overhaul whose final cloture votes were still pending at Edition 6's cutoff. It sets a 150-day window for NEPA lawsuits, limits court-ordered work stoppages, protects issued permits from agency reversal, expands FERC authority over interstate transmission at 230 kV and above, and now goes to the House.
  • Mechanical M&A: Coral Gables–based MasTec has agreed to acquire St. Louis mechanical contractor Murphy Corporation for about $1.25 billion in cash (announced Fri Nov 20) — roughly 1,600 employees, about $775 million in projected 2026 revenue, and a substantial HVAC service and maintenance business aimed at data centers, healthcare and advanced manufacturing.
  • HVAC refrigerants: After the midterms, ACCA, PHCC and HARDI warn more states may follow Washington and New York toward ultra-low-GWP A3 refrigerants such as R-290 (propane) before safety standards, codes and training are ready (ACHR News, Nov 20). Washington's code council voted Oct 30 to allow A3 refrigerants in certain HVAC equipment from May 2027; New York's GWP limits begin phasing out even the new A2L refrigerants in 2027.
  • Florida: Fort Meade's proposed 4.4-million sq. ft. hyperscale data center in Polk County goes back to the City Commission for a fresh vote after design revisions (public meeting Thu Nov 19; final vote hoped for Jan 12; horizontal construction targeted for Q2 2027; 4,000 construction jobs at peak) — and World Electric Supply, one of Sharma's two material suppliers, has leased 274,040 sq. ft. in Lakeland for its third and final regional distribution center.
  • Electrical spotlight (carried from Friday): Resideo First Alert CX6T Advanced AI Camera + Resideo One portal — an 8MP/4K turret camera with edge AI, natural-language video search, sound detection and built-in deterrence, managed across sites in the new Resideo One portal, introduced Thu Nov 19, 2026 at Resideo's CONNECT event; available through security distributors in the coming months.
  • HVAC spotlight (carried from Friday): Viessmann Vitocal 300-G intelligent energy (ie) — an indoor ground-source (brine-to-water) heat pump on natural refrigerant R290 (propane), 6–16 kW, SCOP up to 5.5, 70 °C supply temperature, on Viessmann's new One Base Intelligence control platform; announced by the manufacturer Mon Nov 2, 2026, shown publicly for the first time at GET Nord in Hamburg Nov 19–21, with deliveries starting Nov 30, 2026.
