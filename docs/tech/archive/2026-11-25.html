At a Glance

  • Data centers: Gilbane has broken ground on the $74.5 million Empire AI supercomputing center at the University at Buffalo — a research facility exempt from New York's statewide data center moratorium because the July permit pause carves out research and education use; the center will initially draw up to 5 MW, run on hydropower, and reuse its computing heat to warm campus buildings through the university's chilled-water plant, geothermal wells and heat pumps. Substantial completion is targeted for late 2027 (Construction Dive, Tue Nov 24).
  • Contracts: A Frampton Construction executive's Construction Dive opinion piece (Tue Nov 24) names the four contract provisions he will never sign — unknown conditions priced as defined scope, responsibility without authority (including design responsibility for documents the contractor did not produce), notice and liquidated-damages terms the field team cannot realistically meet (24- to 36-hour notice windows against a 21-day template norm), and liability that exceeds insurance and financial capacity. It is a GC's list, but every item on it flows down to subcontractors.
  • HVAC business: ACHR News (Tue Nov 24) profiles California company Vayu's group-buy model: homeowners planning heat pump replacements are grouped, volume discounts negotiated, and installs scheduled into contractors' slow shoulder seasons — Vayu handles sizing, permitting, sourcing and customer management ("we do everything but the installation") and partners with roughly one in ten contractors it screens. Separately, EffectiV's BREEZ architectural duct diffusers won the 2027 AHR Expo Innovation Award in Ventilation, with its free SelectiV design platform a Software finalist (ACprosite, Tue Nov 24).
  • Florida projects: Three South Florida items on Tue Nov 24 — in Coconut Creek, a joint venture secured $96 million in construction financing for Lutea at Mainstreet, a 404-unit multifamily community starting construction in December (PR Newswire); in Brickell, Miami, Namdar Group closed a $345 million construction loan for a 42-story, 1,008-unit tower at 250 SW 7th Street (Florida YIMBY); and in Palm Beach Gardens, Gatsby Florida and Hedrick Brothers broke ground on The Palm, a 225,000 sq. ft. Class AA office building backed by about $118.6 million in construction financing (EIN Presswire).
  • Electrical spotlight (new today): ABB Intelleqt smart low-voltage motor — described by ABB as the world's first low-voltage motor with intelligence integrated at the factory: built-in sensing, edge computing and connectivity delivering real-time operating data from its first rotation, with early fault detection that ABB says can cut unplanned downtime by up to 50%. Launched Tue Nov 24, 2026 at the SPS trade fair in Germany; now in a customer co-creation phase, broader rollout planned for 2027 — price not published.
  • HVAC spotlight (new today): Differ Power D Series "9-in-1" solar-storage heat pump — a residential air-to-water heat pump with a 6 kW hybrid inverter built into the unit, combining heating, cooling and hot water with PV inversion, battery management, backup power and home energy management in one box; R290 (propane) refrigerant, three sizes from 12.14 to 16.04 kW, COP up to 4.82 and hot water up to 75 °C. Unveiled Tue Nov 24, 2026 at Spain's Genera trade show for the European market — no U.S. availability or pricing announced.
