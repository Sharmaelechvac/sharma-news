At a Glance

  • Builder confidence: the NAHB/Wells Fargo Housing Market Index fell four points to 25 in November — the lowest reading since April 2012 — in data released Tue Nov 17. All three components fell; buyer traffic dropped to 16, its lowest in 15 years outside the start of the pandemic, and 46% of builders cut prices, a post-Covid high, at an average cut of 6%.
  • Update — Brooklyn collapse: on Tue Nov 17 the city ordered portions of the excavation beside 1321 Flatbush Ave backfilled, paused debris removal for an independent forensic engineer, and ordered shoring at neighbouring 1325 Flatbush. ENR's records review says whether the collapsed building was actually underpinned is still unclear, and the required monitoring data is not in the public record.
  • Safety credentials: a New York safety-training provider, RedCode Inc., has been indicted for allegedly selling Site Safety Training and OSHA cards for $500 each with no training; its cards — held by about 2,000 workers — have been invalidated and the company is closed.
  • Florida: Lee County commissioners awarded Wright Construction Group a $47.1 million contract on Tue Nov 17 for Alico Road Phase 1A in southern Lee County, with construction expected to begin in early January 2027.
  • Electrical spotlight product (new today): GE Vernova HYpact-145g compact switchgear — a 145 kV, 40 kA SF6-free hybrid switchgear using g3 gas in place of SF6, at least 50% smaller than a conventional air-insulated substation, launched Tue Nov 17, 2026 in Cologne and available to order now. (Held as not confirmed in Edition 3 because the announcement landed after that edition's 5:00 AM ET close; it sits inside today's window.)
  • HVAC spotlight product (new today): Carrier ClimaVision 3-MyStat Pro Pack — a packaged light-commercial solution combining Carrier rooftop equipment, connected controls, cloud technology and AI from Carrier's 75F acquisition, announced Tue Nov 17, 2026; orders open this year, shipments planned for early 2027.
