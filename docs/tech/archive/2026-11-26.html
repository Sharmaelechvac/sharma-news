At a Glance

  • Data center power: Texas regulators have authorized VoltaGrid to expand the gas-fired plant serving Vantage Data Centers' $25 billion Frontier AI campus near Abilene from 709 MW to 2,582 MW — 620 Jenbacher engines in 10 independent power blocks for a 1,200-acre campus of 10 data centers totalling 3.7 million sq. ft. and 1.4 GW of critical computing capacity (ENR, Wed Nov 25; the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality approval itself is dated Nov 19).
  • Permitting — UPDATE: ACHR News (Wed Nov 25) adds the contractor view of the Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act the Senate passed Nov 19 by 62–33 (first reported in Edition No. 9): SMACNA, ABC, AGC and NECA back the bill's one-year / two-year NEPA review clocks, 150-day legal-challenge window and protection against agencies revoking issued permits — and the House path is unsettled, with amendment fights that could push final action into 2027.
  • Construction and tech: Construction Dive (Wed Nov 25) rounds up three apartment groundbreakings — a 342-unit Mayo Clinic-adjacent project in Rochester, Minnesota, a 134-unit mixed-income redevelopment in Brooklyn with an all-electric system, and a $22.5 million office-to-residential conversion in Atlantic City — and interviews DPR's healthcare co-leader on how AI, layout robots and RFID tracking are changing healthcare builds. Separately, the New York AG has extended the claims deadline in the $1.4 million Alba Services workers' comp restitution fund to Dec 18 (Construction Dive, Wed Nov 25).
  • Workforce: Bring Back the Trades will run four Skills Expos across four New England states in 2027 — the most it has ever held in one year — with HVACR distributor F.W. Webb Co. continuing as title sponsor (ACHR News, Wed Nov 25).
  • Florida projects: In Miami, curtain-wall glass installation is advancing on the 748,975 sq. ft. City of Miami Administration Building at Miami Freedom Park (Moss Construction is GC; completion spring 2028), and in North Miami Beach the developers of the completed 254-unit Nexo Residences have fully repaid the $73 million construction loan (Florida YIMBY, Wed Nov 25). As a brief: FDOT has scheduled an approximately $4.8 million U.S. 27 milling, resurfacing and signalization project in Hendry County (Okeechobee Times, Wed Nov 25).
  • Electrical spotlight (new today): EVE Energy AIDC full-scenario energy storage portfolio — a three-layer storage architecture for AI data centers spanning source-grid, power-distribution and cabinet-level backup, headlined by the S5-1P, a 20-ft, 5 MWh, 1P high-rate container system with a dedicated 315 Ah frequency-regulation cell, the Fortron cabinet delivering 660 kW for five minutes, and a rack-level BBU+CBU system that smooths AI load swings across a 1-millisecond-to-10-second window. Global debut Wed Nov 25, 2026 at RE+ 2026 in Las Vegas — price not published.
  • HVAC spotlight (new today): Afore R290 heat pump series — a residential air-to-water heat pump line on propane (R290) in three sizes from 8.03 to 11.75 kW, heating from -25 °C, delivering water up to 75 °C, pairing an outdoor monobloc unit (Mitsubishi full-DC inverter compressors, 36–47 dB(A) at one metre) with an indoor hydraulic module, and integrating with solar, battery storage and EV charging through an AI-enabled energy management system. Launched Wed Nov 25, 2026 for the European market — no U.S. availability or pricing announced.
