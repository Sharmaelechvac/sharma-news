At a Glance

  • Data centers: U.S. data center cancellations more than doubled in Q3 2026 — 16 projects stopped, up from 7 in Q3 2025, representing about $64 billion in planned investment; 11 of the 16 were stopped directly by local governments (7 formal denials, 4 zoning bans). Since 2024, Relae's tracker counts 70 projects blocked, withdrawn or stalled, representing about $271 billion (Construction Dive, Mon Nov 23, reporting Relae data released Nov 17).
  • Safety: OSHA has proposed $1,586,559 in penalties against South Dakota bridge contractor Grangaard Construction after a crane's suspended concrete segment contacted an energized 34.5-kV power line on Mar 17, hospitalizing a worker — 32 citation items including alleged willful failures on power-line clearance planning, crane overloads and fall protection; the company contested on Sep 29 and the case remains open (ENR, Mon Nov 23).
  • HVAC market: Heat pump adoption in new homes has climbed from roughly 28% in 2022 to about 48% in 2026 (partial-year data) across more than 2.1 million HERS-rated homes, per Ekotrope's September 2026 Behind the Blueprint report (ACHR News, Mon Nov 23). Separately, ACHR News reports Carrier, Trane Technologies and Lennox posted improved Q3 revenue in residential and commercial, with data centers driving commercial demand — and continued price increases likely for contractors.
  • Florida projects: In Lakeland (Polk County, in Sharma's territory), Florida Polytechnic University's board approved (Nov 18) a Gilbane public-private partnership to build a six-story, 159,172 sq. ft., 601-bed residence hall plus a 27,517 sq. ft., 740-seat dining facility — construction expected to begin spring 2027, completion fall 2028 (REBusinessOnline, Mon Nov 23). In Miami, a $150 million construction permit (issued Nov 19) covers the 42-story, 544-unit 1775 Biscayne tower (Florida YIMBY, Mon Nov 23).
  • Electrical spotlight (new today): Briggs & Stratton SimpliPHI 16 energy storage battery — a 16 kWh lithium iron phosphate (LFP) battery that scales to 512 kWh, rated for 8,000 cycles at 90% depth of discharge with a minimum 96% round-trip efficiency, an integrated BMS and Wi-Fi gateway, and a 12-year parts warranty that includes labor and travel stipends; introduced Fri Nov 20, 2026, available for pre-order, price not disclosed.
  • HVAC spotlight (new today): ECO2 Systems Sanco 40kW Heat Pump — a 40 kW (136,520 BTU/h) air-to-water heat pump for commercial domestic hot water using natural CO2 (R744) refrigerant, at a COP of 5.0, with 208/230 V or 460 V three-phase electrical connections; unveiled Tue Nov 24, 2026, with the company describing it as approaching launch — no availability date or pricing specified.
