At a Glance

  • Florida approvals: The City of Miami Commission has approved Midtown Park, a $2 billion, 1.2-million sq. ft. mixed-use master plan on five acres in Midtown Miami — up to 924 residential units, more than 120,000 sq. ft. of retail and restaurant space and about 60,000 sq. ft. of office space (developers Rosso Development and Midtown Development announced the Major Use Special Permit approval on Nov 24; Florida YIMBY, Fri Nov 27). Phase 1 is a 28-story, 280-residence condominium tower, with completion anticipated in 2029.
  • EV charging and storage: Schneider Electric launched the EVlink Pro DC 720 ultra-fast charger (up to 720 kW, 20%–80% in about 15 minutes, up to 12 vehicles at once) and the Schneider Boost Pro battery storage system (scalable 200 kWh to 2 MWh) in Hong Kong on Fri Nov 27 — a commercial charging-plus-storage package aimed at fleets and depots, with a first local commercial deployment already operating.
  • Tools: Krohne's Optisonic 6300 P — a battery-powered, clamp-on ultrasonic flowmeter that measures liquids without cutting pipe or stopping the process, across pipe sizes from ½-in to 160-in — was featured by ACHR News on Fri Nov 27. It is a verification and troubleshooting tool for hydronic work, not a new-product launch claim.
  • HVAC business: The 2026 ACCA / Farmington Consulting Group Contractor of the Future study (1,150+ contractors, reported Fri Nov 27) puts the average contractor's net profit at 7% against 21% for high performers; 56% earn under 10% net and 18% are not profitable. High performers train technicians 70+ hours a year against a 41-hour average, and only 27% of installs get a room-by-room load calculation.
  • Refrigerants: Bosch Home Comfort has moved about 70% of its standard heat pump portfolio to R290 (propane), including a new R290 water-to-water heat pump introduced at Chillventa 2026 with first units expected in early 2027 (NaturalRefrigerants.com, Fri Nov 27) — the European low-GWP transition continuing to set the direction North American rules are debating.
  • Florida projects: In Hialeah, a $51.2 million Centennial Bank construction loan has closed for the expansion of the Terraces of Hialeah skilled nursing facility — growing from 55,559 to about 114,865 sq. ft. over an estimated 18 months of construction with the facility partly operational throughout (Florida YIMBY, Fri Nov 27). In Naples, the $26 million Naples Pier rebuild has its midway pavilion framed with decking and roofing under way, targeting completion in mid-2027. In Jacksonville, a 5,171 sq. ft. waterfront restaurant is planned for Riverfront Plaza (opening anticipated 2028).
  • Electrical spotlight (carried from Friday): Innomotics GEN C generator — a compact, three-phase synchronous generator with brushless excitation, designed specifically for data center power supply and engineered for backup and continuous duty with UPS and other demanding loads, Class H stator insulation and an unlimited operating-hours design. Introduced Thu Nov 26, 2026 at SPS 2026 in Nuremberg — price not published.
  • HVAC spotlight (carried from Friday): A. O. Smith CAE Series tankless instant water heater — an on-demand electric water heater positioned as the smallest in its class, in 3.5 kW and 5.5 kW variants, with a next-generation heat exchanger engineered for hard-water resistance and stable-temperature delivery. Unveiled Thu Nov 26, 2026 in Makati, Philippines, for the Philippine market — no U.S. availability or pricing announced.
