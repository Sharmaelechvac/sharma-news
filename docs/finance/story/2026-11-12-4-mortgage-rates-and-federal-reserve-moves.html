Context note: the October CPI itself was released Tuesday, Nov 10 (CPI +0.4% m/m vs. 0.3% expected; core +0.3% vs. 0.2% expected; year-over-year figures withheld because of the 2025 government shutdown) — just before this window. Everything below is the in-window (Nov 11) market reaction and Fed repricing to that print, as the mission asked for.

Item 4A — “Mortgage rates jump sharply higher, after easing for four straight weeks. Here's why” - Source: CNBC (Diana Olick), via Tokyo Wire syndication of the CNBC article — https://review.tokyo-wire.com/2026/11/11/mortgage-rates-jump-sharply-higher-after-easing-for-four-straight-weeks-heres-why/ - Published: November 11, 2026 (CNBC page shown as 7:38 AM EST Nov 11 in a same-day screenshot of the article) - Key numbers: Mortgage News Daily's average 30-year fixed rate rose 12 bp on Tuesday to 7.30%, the largest one-day jump in more than a month, ending four straight weeks of declines; MBA's weekly average 30-year rate fell to 7.21% from 7.30% for the week ended Nov 6 (lowest since mid-September, and the survey predates Tuesday's jump); total applications +6.2% that week; purchase applications +8% week-over-week but −18% year-over-year; refinance applications +5% week-over-week but −57% year-over-year; the FHA rate moved back below 7%, more than 20 bp under the conforming rate. (Barchart's Nov 11 wrap reports the same MBA release as purchase +7.5% / refi +4.5% — a small discrepancy vs. CNBC's +8% / +5%, flagged in Could not verify.) - Why it matters to Sharma: Buyers who were re-engaging at 7.21% just saw daily rates back at 7.30% with a hike priced in, which supports builders stretching starts and leaning harder on rate buydowns that squeeze sub pricing.

Item 4B — “Hot US October inflation hardens the case for a December Fed hike” - Source: Wealth Professional, reporting Reuters — https://www.wealthprofessional.ca/news/industry-news/hot-us-october-inflation-hardens-the-case-for-a-december-fed-hike/393990 - Published: November 11, 2026 (date on page; no time shown) - Key numbers: Traders moved to fully price a December hike of at least 25 bp; odds of a 50 bp move climbed to 11.8% from no consideration a session earlier (Reuters/CME FedWatch; December hike odds had stood at 84% on Monday); Fed Governor Waller said pre-release he would support a December hike and that a half-point move should be considered if conditions worsened; Dallas Fed President Logan said the Fed needs at least two more quarter-point increases; the 2-year Treasury yield rose ~18.9 bp to 4.736% and the 10-year leapt to 4.99% from 4.88%. - Why it matters to Sharma: A fully priced December hike — with a second Logan-style hike openly discussed — means construction-loan and floorplan costs for your builders are set to rise again before year-end, not ease.

Item 4C — “A Fed Governor Called the Latest Inflation Data 'Borderline Disturbing.' A Half-Point Hike is Now Firmly on the Table.” - Source: International Business Times — https://feeds.ibtimes.co.za/fed-officials-warn-after-hotter-october-cpi-3809644 - Published: November 11, 2026, 10:19 AM ET (in window) - Key numbers: CME FedWatch on Wednesday: 86.8% probability of a 25 bp December hike and 13.2% of a 50 bp hike — i.e., no pause priced, only the size in question (compare Item 4B's earlier 11.8% half-point snapshot; the half-point share rose during Nov 11); Chicago Fed President Goolsbee called the data “borderline disturbing” on PBS; Waller: hiking only 25 bp when conditions warrant more could put Fed credibility “at risk.” - Why it matters to Sharma: With markets split only between +25 and +50 for Dec 8–9, Sharma should underwrite Q1 builder volume and its own line-of-credit costs assuming higher, not flat, rates.

Item 4D — “Stocks Surge on AI Demand Optimism” (market wrap with Fed pricing) - Source: Barchart — https://www.barchart.com/story/news/8783609/stocks-surge-on-ai-demand-optimism - Published: November 11, 2026, 10:15 AM CST = 11:15 AM ET (in window) - Key numbers: “The markets are now pricing in a 100% chance of a +25 bp rate hike at the FOMC's next meeting on December 8–9” (read alongside Items 4B/4C: 100% for at least 25 bp, with a slice of that probability on 50 bp); no cash Treasury trading Nov 11 (Veterans Day); December 10-year T-note futures fell to a 1.5-week low. - Why it matters to Sharma: Bond futures falling on a holiday-thin session with hikes fully priced keeps upward pressure on the mortgage rates your builders' buyers must qualify at.
