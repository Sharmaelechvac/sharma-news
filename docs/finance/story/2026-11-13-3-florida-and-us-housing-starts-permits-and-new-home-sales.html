Item 3A — "Existing-Home Sales Decreased 5.5% in October, According to NAR Report" (the scheduled 10:00 AM ET, Nov 12 release — ACTUAL results) - Source: National Association of REALTORS® (primary release) — https://www.nar.realtor/newsroom/existing-home-sales-decreased-5-5-percent-october-nar-report (GlobeNewswire mirror timestamped Nov 12, 2026, 10:00 AM ET) - Published: November 12, 2026, 10:00 AM ET — verified on the NAR page itself - Key numbers (actuals, not consensus): Total existing-home sales 3.75 million SAAR, −5.5% MoM, −8.8% YoY — lowest since August 2010 (Reuters consensus had been 3.85M). Median existing-home price (all housing types) $416,700, +0.4% YoY (from $414,900), the 40th straight YoY increase. Inventory 1.59 million units, +1.3% MoM, +4.6% YoY = 5.1 months' supply (4.7 in September; most since November 2015 per Reuters). South region: sales −6.5% MoM to 1.73 million SAAR, −8.0% YoY; median price $360,900, −0.4% YoY. NAR also reported October's average 30-year rate (Freddie Mac) at 7.33%, up from 6.86% in September and 6.25% a year ago, and the Housing Affordability Index at 100.7 vs. 107.3 a year ago. - Why it matters to Sharma: The South is weakening faster than the nation, which points to slower resale-driven work and more buyer competition for the new homes Sharma's builders must sell.

No Florida-specific starts/permits/sales release was published inside the window either; Florida Realtors' Q3 report was released Oct 16. - Why it matters to Sharma: There is no new starts/permits signal to plan crews against until Nov 18 — do not treat the weak NAR resale print as a starts number.
