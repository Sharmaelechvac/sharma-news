Item 1A — Bessent on Sunday television: the Fed "may be making a policy mistake if they over-tighten" — CBS Face the Nation, Sunday, November 22 - Source / links: CBS News transcript via DailyFoxNews, Transcript: Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent on "Face the Nation with Margaret Brennan," Nov. 22, 2026 — https://dailyfoxnews.com/news/1431/transcript-treasury-secretary-scott-bessent-on-face-the-nation-with-margaret ; Bloomberg Government via BGov, Bessent Says Fed Risks Overtightening as Markets Swoon, Nov 22, 2026 — https://news.bgov.com/bloomberg-government-news/bessent-warns-fed-risks-overtightening-as-us-markets-swoon ; InvestingLive, US equity rout: Bessent cautions Fed against overtightening, before its December decision, Nov 22, 2026 — https://investinglive.com/central-banks/us-equity-rout-bessent-cautions-fed-against-overtightening-before-its-december-decision/ - Published: Sunday, November 22, 2026 (inside the window). This is a new statement, distinct from Bessent's Friday CNBC remarks in the prior edition. - Key figures: Bessent said "many people believe that they may be making a policy mistake if they over-tighten," pointing to "very hawkish remarks from the Federal Reserve" and "a substantial tightening in financial conditions over the past few months" (CBS transcript, verbatim). He attributed the selloff to a combination of that tightening, hawkish Fed remarks, and Democrats moving ahead in the polls with a stance on the AI buildout that he said spooked investors — the political attribution is Bessent's view, not an established cause. He did not name any Fed official and did not say what December outcome he wants. In the same interview he addressed the week's bank tech outages, calling them glitches from security patches. The policy backdrop is unchanged: target range 3.75%–4.00% (held October 28, 10–2, both dissenters favouring a hike), and the October minutes record that almost all participants saw further increases as likely. - Why it matters to Sharma: the Treasury Secretary has now argued against overtightening on three successive appearances (CNBC November 13, CNBC November 20, CBS November 22), each time without asking for a specific December outcome. That pattern pressures the 50 bp tail, not the 25 bp base case — and Sunday-evening pricing (Item 1C) moved exactly that way. For Sharma's purposes the signal is narrow: the public fight lowers the odds of the larger hike that would hit mortgage money hardest, while the standard hike Bessent is not opposing remains the planning case into December 9.

Item 1B — Ackman joins the pushback: a December hike would be "another mistake"; adopt a 3% target — X post, Sunday, November 22 - Source / links: CoinStats (Cointelegraph report), December Fed rate decision: Ackman warns of policy mistake, Nov 22–23, 2026 — https://coinstats.app/news/e5a03c6b9c12f38c461084b87b7e9e27fa6063e4d546a0b62e6e981b3a0e8087_December-Fed-rate-decision-Ackman-warns-of-policy-mistake/ ; AlphaMaven (BigGo Finance), Fed December Rate-Hike Odds Surge Past 90% as Ackman, Bessent Warn of Policy Error, Nov 2026 — https://alpha-maven.com/story/hedge-fund/fed-december-rate-hike-odds-surge-past-90-as-ackman-bessent-warn-of-policy-error-biggo-finance - Published: the post was made Sunday, November 22, 2026 (inside the window); the CoinStats write-up carrying it was published early Monday, November 23. - Key figures: Pershing Square CEO Bill Ackman posted on X on November 22 that the Fed was about to make "another mistake" on December 9, that financial conditions have tightened "massively," and that policymakers should consider a 3% inflation goal instead of continuing to raise rates to reach 2%, warning that further hikes could push the U.S. and global economies into recession. He cited structural inflation sources — expanding global military spending, tariff barriers, and large AI-infrastructure and energy capital spending. His 3% proposal is a private investor's argument, not a change in Fed policy; no Fed official has endorsed it. Governor Cook's Friday estimate stands opposite: October 12-month PCE inflation 3.8%, core 3.2% (prior editions). - Why it matters to Sharma: when the Treasury Secretary and a prominent investor make the same argument on the same Sunday, the December debate is no longer about data alone — but neither man votes. The operational read is unchanged from Item 1A: the pressure is on the size and the path after December, not on whether a standard 25 bp hike happens. Do not let weekend commentary soften bid-validity discipline into December 9; Wednesday's PCE print (Item 1C) is the next fact that can actually move pricing.

That is a step down from Friday's CME read of more than 98% for +25 bp (prior edition) — the week's rout, not any new data, did the trimming. The calendar now governs: no FOMC decision this week; the Fed's communications blackout begins at midnight ET Saturday, November 28; New York Fed President John Williams speaks Monday, November 23 at 4:00 PM ET, among the last scheduled chances to signal before the blackout. Wednesday's cluster (all 8:30 AM ET, November 25 unless noted): October PCE — Bloomberg consensus headline +0.3% m/m (3.8% y/y, from 3.6%) and core +0.3% (3.2% y/y, from 3.1%), personal income and spending both +0.4%; Q3 GDP second estimate, consensus 3.5% (advance 3.4%); jobless claims; durable-goods orders (consensus +0.5%); new-home sales at 10:00 AM; EIA petroleum inventories 10:30 AM; Beige Book 2:00 PM. Atlanta Fed GDPNow stood at 3.21% for Q4 as of November 18 (from 3.41% on November 13). Markets are closed Thursday, November 26 for Thanksgiving and close early Friday (equities 1:00 PM ET). - Why it matters to Sharma: the 50 bp scenario — the one outcome that would have repriced builder mortgage money by roughly another quarter-point within weeks — is off the futures board for now, and the remaining question is 25 bp versus a surprise hold. Wednesday's PCE is the last inflation print before December 9 and lands in the thinnest trading week of the year, when moves exaggerate. Any rate-sensitive quote Sharma or its builders issue this week should be written knowing Wednesday 8:30 AM can reprice it before lunch.
