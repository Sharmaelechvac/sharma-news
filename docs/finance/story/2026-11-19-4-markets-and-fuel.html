Item 4B — Oil settles lower on a big EIA build: Brent $91.95 (−1.28%), WTI $83.26 (−3.20%, lowest since August 26) — Wednesday, November 18 - Source / links: Reuters via SRN News, Nov 18, 2026 — https://srnnews.com/oil-prices-fall-as-higher-us-crude-stocks-temper-supply-risks/ ; DTN, Nov 18, 2026 — https://www.dtnpf.com/agriculture/web/ag/news/world-policy/article/2026/11/18/wti-3-us-crude-build-adds-global - Key figures: Brent settled at $91.95/bbl, −$1.19 (−1.28%); WTI settled at $83.26/bbl, −$2.75 (−3.20%), its lowest settlement since August 26, after trading below $82 intraday. The EIA Weekly Petroleum Status Report (Wednesday, 10:30 AM ET, week ended November 13) showed crude inventories +3.4 million barrels to 417.8 million (Reuters poll expected +58,000), gasoline stocks +5.2 million barrels to 215.9 million (expected +1.6 million), and distillates +1.6 million barrels to 104.9 million. Crude stocks remain 3% below the five-year seasonal average and distillates 7% below. Both benchmarks are down about 20% in November. In the wider commodities wrap, natural gas was the only contract to settle higher (+5.11%); sixteen of seventeen tracked contracts fell. - Why it matters to Sharma: the surplus story is now in the official inventory data, not just forecasts — crude, gasoline and diesel feedstocks all built more than expected. That keeps downward pressure on pump diesel and on any supplier fuel surcharge still priced off the September spike.

Item 4C — AAA pump prices: regular $3.7502/gal, diesel $5.9594/gal on Wednesday — both down on the day and the week - Source / link: AAA Fuel Prices, national averages as of Nov 18, 2026 — https://gasprices.aaa.com/?premiumhtml5map_get_ - Key figures: AAA's national average on Wednesday, November 18 was regular gasoline $3.7502/gal (down ~3.2 cents from Tuesday's $3.7818, ~19 cents below a week ago, ~55 cents below a month ago, still ~67 cents above a year ago) and diesel $5.9594/gal (down ~2.5 cents from Tuesday's $5.9848, ~17 cents below a week ago, still ~$2.19 above a year ago). The EIA's weekly survey (week of November 16) put regular at $3.805 and on-highway diesel at $5.937 — diesel down 19 cents on the week in that survey, its third straight weekly fall. - Why it matters to Sharma: diesel holding under $6 for a third day, with EIA's weekly average down 19 cents, is real relief for crew-truck and delivery costs against the late-September peak above $6.50 — and it keeps undercutting surcharges priced off that peak.
