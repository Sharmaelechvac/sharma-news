Numbers first. Monday, November 16, 2026 was a full trading day and a risk-off one. (1) Florida OIR APPROVED the NCCI workers' comp cut — final order published Monday, November 16: −7.4% average for new and renewal policies effective January 1, 2027 (verified live on OIR's newsroom this morning; yesterday's edition reported no order — this is the order landing). (2) Stocks fell a third straight session and the Nasdaq entered correction: S&P 500 7,416.59 (−1.92%), Nasdaq Composite 24,509.76 (−2.49%, now ~10.9% below its October 6 high), Dow 51,608.17 (−1.41%, −739.38 points); Nvidia fell 4.12% to $156.14 ahead of Tuesday's earnings and the chip index lost ~5.3%. (3) Copper broke below $6: COMEX front-month settled −1.35% at $5.9975/lb — down three straight sessions, −4.41% over three sessions, lowest settlement since June 24, 2026; LME official cash copper was $13,375/t, −1.73% on the session. (4) Mortgage rates edged up again: Mortgage News Daily's daily 30-year index was 7.51% on Monday (15-year 7.04%, FHA 7.08%), and the 10-year Treasury eased 3 bp to 5.12%. (5) Florida Realtors' October data (released Monday): single-family closed sales 19,049, −10.1% year-over-year; new pending sales −15.7%; median single-family price $420,000, +2.2%; condo/townhouse median $290,000, −3.3%. (6) Oil settled sharply lower on the surplus outlook: Brent $92.44/bbl (−3.55%, −$3.40), WTI $85.72/bbl (−3.21%, −$2.84), and U.S. retail diesel dropped below $6/gallon for the first time since early September. (7) The Fed gave no rate relief: Chair Warsh's Monday speech was about AI scenarios and said nothing on rates or inflation; Cleveland Fed President Hammack (a 2026 voter) backed slow, steady hikes in smaller increments; late-Monday CME FedWatch snapshots put a December +25 bp hike at up to 95.7% (see Item 1C for the day's range). (8) Empire State manufacturing cooled sharply: 7.8 in November vs 25.5 in October (consensus ~16.7–17.6), with new orders at 3.0 but hiring at its strongest since June 2022 and prices paid easing to 45.9 from 59.5. Today ahead: Census October retail sales at 8:30 AM ET (consensus +0.2%) and the NAHB builder index (consensus 29, flat) — neither is out at the 5:00 AM ET cutoff. There was no new housing-starts/permits release, no HVAC equipment or R-454B price news, no new steel/PVC print, and no named production-builder operating announcement in the window.
