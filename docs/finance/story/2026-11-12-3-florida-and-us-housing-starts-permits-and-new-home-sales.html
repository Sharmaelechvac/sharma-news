Item 3A — “Second Look at Local Housing Markets in October” (NAR preview) - Source: Calculated Risk — https://calculatedrisk.substack.com/p/second-look-at-local-housing-markets - Published: November 11, 2026, 17:04 UTC = 12:04 PM ET (in window) - Key numbers: NAR October existing-home sales are scheduled for Thursday, November 12; consensus 3.87 million SAAR, down from 3.99 million in September; sales in early-reporting local markets fell 13.7% year-over-year (NSA) in October, vs. −5.2% in September (October 2026 had 21 working days vs. 22 in October 2025, so the seasonally adjusted headline will look less weak); the current cycle low is 3.85 million SAAR (Oct 2023); Calculated Risk assesses 2026 existing sales are on track to be the lowest since 1994. - Why it matters to Sharma: A near-cycle-low resale market means fewer move-up buyers feeding new construction and more price competition for the custom/remodel work that fills gaps between production starts.

Item 3C — “Florida Keys Market Pulse — October 2026” - Source: Momentum Realty / Momentum Housing Research (Florida Keys MLS data) — https://movewithmomentum.com/reports/pdf/2026-10-florida-keys-market-pulse.pdf - Published: November 11, 2026 (in window) - Key numbers: Single-family median sold price $1,237,000 (+19.5% YoY, but same-home appreciation only +3.6%/yr); 104 single-family closings (+13.0% YoY); median 98 days on market; 7.5 months of supply; only 3% sold over list; 42% of active listings had cut price as of Nov 11; 52% of listings leaving the market in October failed to sell (147 expired/withdrawn/canceled vs. 135 closed). Caveat: the Keys are a high-end/second-home market, not production-builder Florida. - Why it matters to Sharma: Even in a resilient Florida submarket, 7.5 months of supply and 42% price-cutting signal buyer leverage that builders statewide are answering with incentives and slower starts.
