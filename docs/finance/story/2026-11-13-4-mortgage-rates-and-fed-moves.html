Yes, the Nov 12 PMMS was released inside the window. - Key numbers: 30-year FRM averaged 7.11% as of Nov 12, down from 7.18% the prior week (6.24% a year ago); 15-year FRM averaged 6.45%, down from 6.57% (5.49% a year ago). Fourth consecutive weekly decline; lowest 30-year level in seven weeks (Chief Economist Sam Khater). - Why it matters to Sharma: The weekly survey looks backward over Thu–Wed quotes, so it overstates the relief actually available to builders' buyers right now (see Item 4B).

Item 4B — "Mortgage Rates Definitely Aren't Lower This Week!" (Mortgage News Daily daily index, Nov 12) - Source: Mortgage News Daily — https://www.mortgagenewsdaily.com/markets/mortgage-rates-11122026 - Published: November 12, 2026 (article timestamp 3:17 PM UTC) — verified on the source page; index values corroborated by MND's own rate pages and Nov 12 reporting (e.g., Real Estate News, Nov 12: https://www.realestatenews.com/2026/11/12/existing-home-sales-havent-been-this-slow-since-2010) - Key numbers: MND Daily Index, Nov 12: 30-year fixed 7.42% (+0.12 on the day), 15-year 7.00% (+0.08), 30-year FHA 7.00% (+0.11), jumbo 7.46% (+0.06), VA 7.01% (+0.11). MND notes daily rates are up 0.25% since the last Freddie Mac survey and at the highest in more than 3 weeks, despite Freddie's −0.07% weekly print. - Why it matters to Sharma: Buyers locking loans on Nov 12 faced ~7.4%, not 7.11% — expect builders to lean harder on rate buydowns, which squeezes the margins that fund subcontractor pricing.

Item 4C — CME FedWatch: December hike priced as certain on Nov 12, with 50bp odds climbing during the day - Sources: FXStreet, Nov 12, 2026, 12:52 PM GMT — https://www.fxstreet.com/analysis/dollar-hits-19-month-high-as-usd-jpy-nears-160-and-japan-hints-at-intervention-202611121252 ; Reuters global-markets wrap, Nov 13 (Singapore, crawled 2:08 AM UTC Nov 13 = 9:08 PM ET Nov 12, inside the window) — https://www.reuters.com/world/china/global-markets-global-markets-2026-11-13/ - Key numbers: As of 10:00 AM GMT Nov 12, FedWatch showed roughly 91% odds of a 25bp December hike and ~9% of a 50bp hike (100% for a hike of some size), vs. a week earlier 67% for 25bp and 33% for no hike (a 90.8%/9.2% split was the widely republished morning snapshot). By the late-Nov 12 Reuters wrap, the 50bp probability had risen to 14.5%, up from zero a week ago. The FOMC meets December 8–9; the current target range is 3.75%–4.00%. - Why it matters to Sharma: Markets now treat another hike as locked in, so plan for mortgage rates to stay at or above current levels into the December meeting rather than budgeting for relief.

Item 4D — Fed officials on Nov 12: Hammack (gradual hikes), Williams (more hikes likely), Daly (bias to tighten) - Sources: Reuters via KWSN (Hammack), published Nov 12, 2026, 3:28 PM UTC — https://kwsn.com/2026/11/12/feds-hammack-signals-support-for-gradual-rate-hike-path/ ; Reuters via 94.1 The Duke (Williams), published Nov 13, 1:35 AM UTC = Nov 12, 8:35 PM ET — https://941theduke.com/2026/11/12/feds-williams-says-central-bank-has-work-to-do-to-get-inflation-back-on-target/ ; Barchart market wrap, published Nov 12, 2026, 3:42 PM CT — https://www.barchart.com/story/news/8810395/stocks-settle-lower-on-nvidia-rout-and-higher-crude-and-bond-yi - Key numbers/quotes: Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack (voting member, Ohio State event) backed moving to a restrictive stance in "smaller increments, more frequent, smaller rises" and saw no need for aggressive moves. New York Fed President John Williams said more rate increases are likely — "We still have work to do" — citing a strong economy and persistent inflation. San Francisco Fed President Mary Daly (via Barchart's Nov 12 wrap): "My bias is that further tightening will be needed." The 10-year Treasury yield closed Nov 12 up 11.9bp at 5.111% (3-week high) per Barchart. - Why it matters to Sharma: With even the "gradual" camp endorsing more hikes, builder buydown costs and buyer payment shock are a multi-meeting problem, not a one-month blip.

Item 4E — "US weekly jobless claims increase, but labor market firming" (context for the Fed path) - Source: Reuters — https://www.reuters.com/world/us/us-weekly-jobless-claims-increase-labor-market-firming-2026-11-12/ - Published: November 12, 2026, 1:38 PM UTC (8:38 AM ET) - Key numbers: Initial claims rose 9,000 to 200,000 for the week ended Nov 7 (forecast 195,000); continuing claims fell 20,000 to 1.697 million (week ended Oct 31); claims have held at or below 200,000 since mid-September. - Why it matters to Sharma: A labor market this firm gives the Fed no reason to pause, reinforcing the higher-for-longer rate path that is pressuring Sharma's builders.

Also reported Nov 12 (lower confidence on release timing): Real Estate News (Nov 12) reported MBA mortgage applications rose 6.2% in the week ending Nov 6, with purchase applications up 8% on a seasonally adjusted basis (4% unadjusted in the same article) and refinances +5% week-over-week but −57% year-over-year — a small demand response to the prior week's lower rates.
