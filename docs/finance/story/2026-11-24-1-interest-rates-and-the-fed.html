Williams was flagged in the prior edition as Monday's 4:00 PM ET speaker; this is the speech itself. - Key figures: New York Fed President John Williams, vice chair of the FOMC and a permanent voter, said persistently high inflation "continues to point the US central bank toward tighter monetary policy" and that "we must take actions over time that will ensure that we get inflation back to our 2% goal in a timely manner and on a sustained basis" — "there is still work to do." In the prepared text he called getting inflation back to 2% "100 percent essential" and put PCE inflation at about 3.5%, against the 2% target. His projections: inflation ends 2026 at 3.5%, moderates to just above 2% in 2027 and reaches 2% in 2028; real GDP grows about 2.75% in both 2026 and 2027, an economy he called strong and resilient. To reporters he said he will keep assessing incoming data for the December 8–9 meeting and that on timing "I don't have in my mind a particular timing"; he said he was not surprised by the market's volatility and that it had not dimmed his optimism. The precision point: the prepared text does not mention a rate increase, a specific meeting, or the balance sheet — it quotes the October 28 FOMC statement — while Reuters' reporting of the remarks and his reporter comments reads them as favouring a December hike. Both are stated here because the difference matters: the committee's vice chair endorsed the direction (tighter, over time) without committing the committee to a size or a date. - Why it matters to Sharma: this was the last major scheduled Fed statement before the communications blackout begins at midnight Saturday, November 28, and it came from the leadership, not the hawkish wing. It neither opened the door Bessent and Ackman pushed on Sunday nor slammed it: the base case it supports is the standard 25 bp path, sustained "over time" — Williams' own inflation path (3.5% this year, target not reached until 2028) is an argument for a sequence of hikes, not one-and-done. For Sharma's bid-validity discipline nothing changes: plan on December 9 delivering +25 bp, and treat Wednesday's PCE (Item 1C) as the only remaining fact that can move that before the blackout.
