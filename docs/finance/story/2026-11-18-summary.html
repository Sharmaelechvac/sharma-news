Numbers first. Tuesday, November 17, 2026 was a full trading day and the week's big housing read landed hard. (1) Builder sentiment crashed to a 14-year low: the NAHB/Wells Fargo Housing Market Index fell 4 points to 25 in November (consensus 29, October 29), the lowest since April 2012 — current sales 28 (−6), future expectations 30 (−2), buyer traffic 16 (−2, a 15-year low outside the pandemic); the South regional HMI fell 3 points to 27. 46% of builders cut prices (highest share in the post-Covid period, average cut 6% for an eighth straight month) and 68% offered sales incentives (also a post-Covid high). (2) Stocks fell a fourth straight day: S&P 500 7,340.53 (−1.03%, −76.06, lowest close since late July), Nasdaq Composite 24,328.38 (−0.74%), Dow 50,759.42 (−1.64%, −848.75); every S&P 500 sector fell, and the VIX jumped ~11% to about 25.5. (3) COMEX copper slid a fourth straight session: front-month settled −0.36% at $5.9760/lb (−2.15 cents), −4.76% over four sessions, the lowest settlement since June 24; LME official cash copper was $13,226/t, −1.12%. (4) Mortgage rates eased a touch: Mortgage News Daily's daily 30-year index was 7.48% on Tuesday (−3 bp from Monday's 7.51%; 15-year 7.02%, FHA 7.05%), and the 10-year Treasury par yield eased 2 bp to 5.10%. (5) Retail sales rose 0.2% in October to $748.2 billion (Census, Tuesday 8:30 AM ET, in line with consensus; ex-autos +0.6%), but with CPI up 0.4% the real sales read was negative — and furniture sales fell 2.3%, while building-material and garden dealers rose 0.5%. (6) The Fed stayed hawkish in stereo: St. Louis Fed President Musalem said several rate hikes are likely needed before spring 2027 (real policy rate to 2%–2.5%), while New York Fed President Williams ruled out a 50 bp "big step" in December; December +25 bp is priced at ~95–97% (a hike would take the policy rate to 4.00%–4.25%). (7) After the close, Nvidia beat big: Q3 fiscal 2027 revenue $115.8 billion (+103% YoY), non-GAAP EPS $2.68, Q4 guidance $130 billion — shares rose about 4–5% in after-hours trading. (8) Home Depot reported before the open: Q3 sales $43.4 billion (+4.9%), comps +2.2%, adjusted EPS $3.86 — and the stock still fell 2.6% to $338.39. (9) Oil settled slightly higher on Hormuz escalation: Brent $93.14/bbl (+0.76%), WTI $86.01/bbl (+0.34%); API reported U.S. crude stocks +5.28 million barrels in the week ended November 13, and AAA diesel was $5.98/gallon on Tuesday (regular $3.78). (10) Florida: Central Florida (Stellar MLS, published Tuesday) — October single-family closings 8,869, −4.0% YoY; median $390,000, +1.3%; new construction was 20% of single-family closings. And an Orlando roofer filed Chapter 11: ALPHA Roofing and Sheet Metal LLC (petition November 16, reported Tuesday, Subchapter V, M.D. Fla.). Today ahead, all after the 5:00 AM ET cutoff: Census October housing starts/permits at 8:30 AM ET (WSJ survey: starts 1.25M, +2.4%; permits 1.38M), NAR pending home sales at 10:00 AM ET (consensus −1.5%), EIA petroleum at 10:30 AM ET, Lowe's earnings before the open, and the October FOMC minutes at 2:00 PM ET. There was no new steel or PVC price print, no new R-454B price issue, and no new CES news in the window.
