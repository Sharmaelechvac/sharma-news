Item 7A — Lowe's Q3: sales $22.76B (+9.3%), comps +1.8%, adjusted EPS $3.10 — including $0.34 from $250M in tariff refunds; margin guidance trimmed — Wednesday, November 18, before the open - Source / links: Zacks, Nov 18, 2026 — https://www.zacks.com/stock/news/3031933/lowes-q3-earnings-beat-estimates-on-tariff-refunds-pro-strength ; Motley Fool earnings call transcript, Nov 18, 2026 — https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/11/18/lowes-low-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/ - Published: Wednesday, November 18, 2026 (inside the window). Quarter ended October 30, 2026. - Key figures: Net sales $22.76 billion, +9.3% year-over-year (consensus ~$22.38 billion), driven by the Foundation Building Materials and Artisan Design Group acquisitions; comparable sales +1.8% — a sixth straight positive quarter — with online sales +17.4% and strength in Pro and home services. Adjusted diluted EPS $3.10, +1.3% (consensus $2.86); GAAP diluted EPS $2.98; net earnings $1.67 billion. The adjusted EPS includes a +$0.34 benefit from ~$250 million in pretax IEEPA tariff refunds — without it, adjusted EPS would have been ~$2.76, below consensus. Gross margin fell 124 bp to 32.9% on fuel costs, transportation pressures and acquisition dilution. Full-year fiscal 2026 guidance: sales raised to ~$92.5 billion (from ~$92 billion) and comps to ~+0.5% (from flat), but operating margin trimmed to ~11.1% (from 11.2%) and adjusted EPS held at ~$12.25. Lowe's operated 1,762 stores at quarter-end. - Why it matters to Sharma: read past the beat — Lowe's core quarter was carried by a one-time tariff refund while its gross margin compressed 124 bp on fuel and transport costs, the same squeeze Sharma faces. Pro demand is genuinely growing (comps +1.8%, online +17.4%), which supports the repair-and-pro channel even as new construction slows.
